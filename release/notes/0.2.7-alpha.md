Patch notes for 0.2.7-alpha. This build runs on Diablo II: Resurrected 3.4 and 3.3.

## Loot Filters

- Seven loot filters ship, from Starter (the default) to Endgame, including the new Semi strict and Ultra strict.
- Esc > Loot Filter now holds Pyre Loot Filter's profile and toggles, in place of D2R's own filters.
- The old single filter no longer ships, so each loot filter is listed once.

## Items and Stash

- Ctrl+click sends map and bossing items to their stash tab from any tab, and plays the item's sound.
- Ctrl+click or Shift+right-click on a big Gems, Materials, Runes, Maps or Bossing stack takes up to 50.
- Shift+right-click on a Gems, Materials or Runes tab item takes it out to the inventory.
- Gems, Materials and Runes stash tabs now reach 255 on installs that kept an old config.
- The stash gold cap is now 25,000,000, shared by all shared stash pages.
- The inventory lock now locks only charms in the charm rows.
- Fixed Shift+Click with an Almanac to identify all inventory and Cube items.
- Fixed map orbs bought from Anya failing to transfer, and stray color markers in their names.

## Skills and Mercenaries

- The Horadric Navigator and Almanac show in the skill picker.
- Melee attacks reach as far as in Project Diablo 2 again, and Sacrifice reaches one step farther.
- Leap Attack and Blade Shield no longer wear out weapons.
- Mercenaries and pets run back to you when they fall behind.
- The Act 1 mercenary's Strafe restarts the bow draw for each extra arrow.
- The Act 2 Might mercenary now uses Thorns instead, at Project Diablo 2 skill levels.
- The Holy Freeze mercenary is no longer for hire.

## Features and Fixes

- Item Roll Ranges and Controller QOL options are in Options > Gameplay.
- Updated Cartographer to 0.1.2.
- Fixed map bosses not spawning.
