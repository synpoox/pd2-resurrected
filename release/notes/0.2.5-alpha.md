Patch notes for 0.2.5-alpha. This build runs on Diablo II: Resurrected 3.4 and 3.3.

## Items

- Raised the Gems, Materials and Runes stash tabs to stacks of up to 255.
- Let throwing weapons, javelins and quivers drop with sockets, as in PD2.
- Added PD2's rare chance for an imbued Phase Blade to become the Armageddon Blade.
- Stopped Embersworn from dropping from the unique roll, matching PD2.
- Gheed in a map event has a 1 in 200 chance to sell Kadala's Heirloom.
- A stackable bought from a vendor now joins a held stack of the same item, as in PD2.
- Uniques bought from the map event Gheed now arrive identified.
- Charged skills with no charges left stay on the skill list and keep their hotkey, as in PD2
- Slow on items no longer slows the prime evils and other bosses PD2 protects, as in PD2.
- Vendors now refuse what PD2 vendors refuse: gems, runes, most stackable items and Uber maps.
- A quest drop stops for everyone once the quest is done in the game, as in PD2.
- You can carry up to 5,000,000 gold at any level, as in PD2.
- The Skeleton Key Unlimited opens locked chests and doors without using up a key.
- A corrupted item's tooltip shows the stats its corruption added or changed in red.
- A map sent to a full Maps tab now stays in your inventory instead of vanishing into hidden storage.
- A map put into the stash now tops up the stack of the same map first.

## Changed

- Gave aura-enchanted bosses PD2's eight monster auras, capped at level 13.
- Shorten the wait before you can use a portal again to 0.6 seconds instead of 3 seconds, as in PD2.

## Skills

- Made Valkyries and revived monsters act sooner, and large revived monsters fit through tight gaps.
- Melee attacks reach further as in PD2: monsters by 1, players by half their weapon's range and 1 more against a moving target.
- Redemption no longer eats the corpses of player summons and mercenaries, as in PD2.
- Summoned minions act sooner after they appear, as in PD2.
- An aura no longer changes the speed of a Charge or Lucion Dash already under way, as in PD2.
- Sacrifice kills now make the corpse explode, damaging nearby enemies as in PD2.
- Blood Golem heals itself and you for its listed life per hit, as in PD2.
- Raven summons 1 raven per cast, plus 1 more per 10 hard points, as in PD2.
- The character screen's skill Damage for Vengeance, throws, kicks, charge-ups, arrows and Blade Fury is PD2's
- Missile and area hits leech half the life and mana, and Leap Attack a third of the life, as in PD2.
- Changed chance to hit to PD2's formula, which can reach 100% instead of 95%.
- Whirlwind and Blade Dance now hit every 0.24 seconds, twice when dual-wielding, as in PD2.
- Whirlwind and Blade Dance always spin through a target in reach and end just past it, as in PD2.
- Chilling Armor's ice shard now falls on the ranged attacker, as in PD2.
- Sacrifice's life cost is lowered by your damage reduction, as in PD2.
- Grim Ward works on hard corpses such as skeletons too, as in PD2.
- Inferno and Arctic Blast fire their full line again at each start while you keep channelling, as in PD2.
- Jab, Inferno, Arctic Blast and Blade Fury animate faster, with PD2's shorter frames.
- Blessed Hammers and other missiles no longer set off Sacrifice corpse explosions.
- Split Throw's attack speed buff lasts for the whole throw and ends with it, as in PD2.
- The character sheet's chance to hit now shows PD2's chance, up to 100%.
- Dragon Talon and Dragon Tail kicks deal PD2's base damage, from your gear, dexterity and boots
- Hunger works on hard corpses such as skeletons too, as in PD2.

## Characters

- Added the max-level aura on level 99 characters.
- Running no longer drops your defense to 0: monsters must roll to hit you, as in PD2.

## Maps

- Stock the map event's Gheed with up to 5 random uniques and keep him standing still.

## Changes

- Classic/LoD characters play the full mod; move SharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i out of the save folder first.

## Features

- Gold within a few yards of you is picked up automatically outside town, as in PD2.

## Monsters

- Monsters steal a sixteenth of the mana they did, and Mana Burn bosses drain far less mana.
- Werewolf and Werebear no longer block your other skills for a moment after you shift.
- Oblivion Knights and the void knights cast PD2's monster curses, not the Necromancer's.
- Baal's throne waves come 5 seconds after his taunt instead of 10, as in PD2.
- New HD models and mercenary portraits for the Dark and Light Ascendant, by RuffnecKk.
