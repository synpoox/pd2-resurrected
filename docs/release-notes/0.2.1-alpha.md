**Move every item out of your Charm Inventory before updating, or your character may be bricked.**

Patch notes for 0.2.1-alpha. This build runs on Diablo II: Resurrected 3.4 and 3.3.

## Inventory and charms

- The inventory is now 10x8 as in PD2. The Charm Inventory is gone: empty it into your inventory or stash before updating.
- Charms work only in inventory rows 5-8, as in PD2. Charms in rows 1-4 are tinted red.
- You can carry a second unique charm of the same name, but two of them in rows 5-8 give no stats until one is moved out.
- The launcher removes the old Charm Inventory plugin (d2rl-charm-inv). If you installed the mod by hand, delete d2rl-charm-inv.dll and .mpq from d2rloader/plugins.

## Maps

- PD2's old map items, affixes and recipes are replaced by Cartographer maps: Tier 1, 2 and 3, rare maps with many affixes, and PD2's map recipes and drops.
- 20 new maps, one for each Cartographer tileset, each with its own icon. Maps have more monsters, uniques and PD2 map bosses.
- Normal maps with the same stats stack up to 50. Using a stack opens one map and leaves the rest.
- Map events run on every map: Dark Wanderer, Mendeln, Treasure Fallen, Gheed and the Invaders are back.
- Anya in Hell gives you 3 random Tier 1 maps with her reward.
- Cube a rare map with a class invader ear for extra drops and two class modifiers.

## Uber bosses

- Diablo Clone, Lucion, Rathma, the Temple of the Triune and the Bitter Depths have their own small enclosed arenas.
- The Uber Tristram key opens D2R's Tristram arena.
- Uber keys open their areas again, and uber ingredients drop again from Hell Mephisto, Diablo and Baal.
- The Sacrifice Altar opens a panel in the Temple: place a corrupted amulet and confirm to Desecrate it. Once per game, as in PD2.

## Items and combat

- Items with replenish charges (Enigma, Oath and others) regain their charges over time.
- Weapons lose durability half as often, and ethereal items keep their full durability.
- Rare items roll an even spread of affix counts. Class items roll skill bonuses from the whole class tree.
- Unique, rare and crafted quivers can be upgraded to their next tier in the cube.
- Shrines work as in PD2. Open wounds stacks, and Holy Sword parries with a two-handed sword.
- Multiple Shot, Dark Pact, Blessed Hammer, the hydras and many summons work again.
- Socketed gems and runes no longer apply their bonuses twice.
- Dodge, avoid and claw block roll while walking and running, as in PD2.

## Controller and interface

- With a controller, all 8 inventory rows can be used, and hold LB and press A to put down one item of a stack.
- Controller QOL Updates (PyreFly) and a Potion Counter now come with the mod, and a DPS counter shows above the life globe.
- The legacy and HD inventories show PD2's full panel, and the Horadric Cube's stash tabs show its full 4x4 grid.

## Known limits

- Skill-on-cast and skill-on-block procs: Cast Triggers now starts with Controller QoL, but the procs are not yet checked in game (#1522).
- The launcher accepts D2R 3.4 (build 95032) and 3.3 (build 93847). The standalone downloader may warn on 3.3 (toolkit #314); use the launcher there.

## Also in this release

- Many fixes to maps, arenas and bosses: missing names, HD models and animations, loot and portals.
- Alpha: characters saved with PD2's old maps may lose map items. Start fresh characters for this build. Multiplayer is not verified, so test in single player.
