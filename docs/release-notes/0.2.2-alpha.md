Patch notes for 0.2.2-alpha. This build runs on Diablo II: Resurrected 3.4 and 3.3.

## Features

- Mercenaries wear an amulet, two rings, gloves, a belt and boots, and gain their stats.
- The mercenary panel lays out the new gear like the inventory, in HD, legacy and controller.
- The stash gets Maps and Bossing tabs: maps sort by tier and base, boss items by boss, with a working Cube.
- Ctrl+click files a map, uber item or map crafting item on its own stash tab.
- PD2 inventory hotkeys: Ctrl/Shift+right click to drop, stash or Cube, with sounds; configurable.
- Ctrl+right click drops and Ctrl+Shift+right click cubes from any open panel, even with the Cube closed.
- Shift+click identifies with a Tome of Identify, Horadric Almanac or Scroll of Identify; a tome's last charge still needs right-click.
- A padlock in the inventory locks your and your mercenary's equipment against clicks and hotkeys.
- An unidentified map gives +30% Item Quantity: kills roll monster drops again more often.
- Map tooltips list their monsters' immunities, like "Immunities: C120, F120".
- Zone info shows Monsters Remaining; it hides with TAB and in towns.
- Invaders wear their real player-class art and drop exactly the gear they wear, rolled from PD2's invader loot.
- Bind and Summon included: summons and Bind Demon return after a rejoin; skeletons rise without a corpse.
- Dyed items such as Corrupted Annihilus show a red-tinted picture in the inventory.
- Linux: a setup guide for Wine or Proton.

## Controller

- Maps and Bossing stash tabs work on a pad; on Maps, LB/RB pick the base and the left stick click the tier.
- RB + A on a stack-tab stack moves 5 to the inventory; LB + RB + A sends it to the Cube.
- Holding a stack shows an "LB + A Split Stack" hint in the inventory.
- The Lock Inventory padlock works with the pad and keeps focus after A.
- The controller inventory tints the charm rows 5-8, like the mouse layout.
- Stash: Maps and Bossing use the Mats tab frame, Personal reads in full, Stash Search is hidden.
- Stash tabs page right with rebound LT/RT, pads 2-4, DS4 and Steam Input.

## Changes

- Stamina Shrines add +35% movement speed.
- Arrows, bolts and throwing weapons never run out, as in PD2.
- Throwing weapon stacks and gamble prices match PD2.
- Superior items no longer raise vendor prices by their stats; repairs cap at 250,000 gold, as in PD2.
- Map tuning: two renames and new sizes, shapes, densities and PD2 monster lists.
- New art for six unique quivers, other equipment and miscellaneous items; the organ portal returns to Uber Tristram.
- Map uniques use stock or Cartographer icons instead of 40 old PD2 icons.
- Bundled Pyre Loot Filter rev.7, Pyre Stash Search rev.7 and Item Roll Ranges rev.61.

## Fixes

- Quest rewards Potion of Life, Scroll of Resistance and Book of Skill can be used again.
- Act 1, 2 and 5 mercenaries no longer crash the game when you change area after they die.
- Healers cure poison again.
- Big fights and map openings no longer stutter or freeze from logging.
- Larzuk's Puzzlebox, Puzzlepiece and five other rare items drop from monsters.
- Spire of Darkness shadows drop Keys of Terror, Hate and Destruction again.
- Leoric's Passage maps spawn undead instead of cows.
- Map events show their cross on the minimap again.
- Map portals open on every setup.
- Missiles no longer stop at invisible walls in Horazon's Memory.
- Gems, runes and materials keep their full stack count in their stash tabs.
- New Classic and Expansion characters get the right starting gear and inventory size.
- Fixed the Sacrifice altar tooltip, the All Attributes bonus text and Zinthros's name.
- Restored the Palace Cellar Level 1 waypoint name and the Cairn Stone order on the Scroll of Inifuss.
- Dropped Worldstone Shards look like crystals in HD.
- Mercenaries and the Ancients use D2R's inventory settings again.
