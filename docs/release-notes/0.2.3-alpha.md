Patch notes for 0.2.3-alpha. This build runs on Diablo II: Resurrected 3.4 and 3.3.

## Features

- A loot filter builder made from the mod's item data, with a ready Regular filter.
- Lock Inventory also locks the charm rows (inventory rows 5-8).
- Maps and Bossing stash tabs: stacks hold up to 255 and taking one out gives 50.
- Maps and Bossing tabs show a red cell and keep the item on the cursor over a wrong cell.
- Maps sent by Ctrl+click or Shift+right click stack onto a same-map stack first.
- The Materials tab has cells for Worldstone Shards, craft infusions, Puzzleboxes and Puzzlepieces.
- Ready orbs sit beside their plain orbs on the Maps tab; the Bossing key cells are gone.

## Changes

- Vendors no longer buy runes.
- The Holy Freeze Act 2 mercenary is no longer on Greiz's hire list.

## Fixes

- Maps and Bossing tabs: wrong drops are refused, never leave an invisible item on the cursor, and top up full stacks correctly.
- Map currency and boss parts stack onto their cells, whether dropped by hand or sent to the stash.
- Maps and Bossing tabs only count their page once the arrows were sent, and hide when the stash has too few pages.
- Shift+right click stashes like Ctrl+click, and Ctrl+click out of a stash tab tops up stacks.
- Stash affinity works with the Shift+right click stash hotkey.
- Monsters Remaining shows a number in outdoor zones once most of the zone is explored, and reads 50+ instead of 25+.
- The Act 1 mercenary uses crossbows and quivers, and the Act 2 mercenary uses spears and polearms.
